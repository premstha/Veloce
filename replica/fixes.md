## 1. Top Themes: What Riders Hate & What is Missing

### Theme A: "Basic Analytics Paywalled / Miles Gamification"
- **Problem:** Riders resent having to pay ₹99/mo or earn "Miles" tokens just to check their bike's cost per km or parts service intervals.
- **Evidence:** 3 reviews across Play Store and Reddit.
- **Our Fix:** 100% free analytics forever. No Miles currency. Cost per km and spare parts health are open to all.

### Theme B: "No Group Map / Individual Tracking Only"
- **Problem:** Group rides are difficult because each rider sends a separate link. Nobody knows who is leading, who is lagging, or where everyone is.
- **Evidence:** App Store review ("We ride in a group of 8... single live map").
- **Our Fix:** The **Group Ride Room** — one live map for the entire group with Lead, Sweep, and Lagging indicators.

### Theme C: "Battery Drain & Patchy Network Drops"
- **Problem:** GPS tracking in remote ghats loses data or drains phone battery completely.
- **Evidence:** Play Store and App Store reviews.
- **Our Fix:** Offline-first caching with IndexedDB, client-generated IDs, and adaptive GPS polling (slower interval when stationary).
