## The Problem

**Problem 1: Basic analytics locked behind subscriptions**

Most bike tracking apps make you pay ₹99/month just to see how much your bike actually costs to ride. Service intervals, fuel efficiency, repair history—all behind a paywall or a gamified "Miles" currency. Riders shouldn't have to buy premium just to understand their own bike.

**Problem 2: Group rides are fragmented**

When your crew goes out, everyone sends a separate tracking link. Nobody knows who's leading, who's lagging, or if someone has stopped. You're riding together but tracking alone.

---

## How It Works

**Step 1: Log Your Bike**

Add your motorcycle, pick the model, and we infer service intervals and fuel tank size. You own all your data—export it anytime as CSV.

**Step 2: Track What Matters**

Every time you fill up, log it. Add repair costs. We calculate your real cost per km in the background. No subscription required.

**Step 3: Ride Together**

When you go out with your group, open a Group Ride Room and share the link. Everyone appears on one live map with speed, lag, and lead indicators. See who's ahead, who needs a break, who's running low on fuel.

## Features

### ✅ 100% Free Analytics
See your bike's cost per km broken down by fuel (%, ₹), repairs (%, ₹), and parts. Compare across your motorcycles. Export anytime.

**Why:** RoadBee charges ₹99/mo for this. We don't believe in paywalls for basic insights.

### ✅ Live Group Ride Room
One map for your entire group. Real-time speeds, distance traveled, lagging riders, and SOS alerts. No fragmented links.

**Why:** Group rides are broken in every tracking app. This is the feature we built from scratch.

### ✅ Offline-First Logging
Riding in the ghats with no signal? Log fuel, expenses, and track points offline. When you're back online, everything syncs automatically.

**Why:** Most tracking apps drain battery or lose data in remote areas. We cache everything client-side.

### ✅ Service & Parts Health
Track every part replacement, service date, and warranty expiry. Get reminders before they fail, not after you're stranded.

**Why:** Maintenance history is scattered across WhatsApp groups and mechanic notebooks. We organize it.

### ✅ Club & Community Rides
Join local riding clubs, see their posted rides, and meet other riders in your city. All free to join.

**Why:** Riding groups form offline, but there's no central place to discover them online.

### ✅ Export Everything
Download all your data as CSV anytime. Your logs, your club membership, your group ride history. No lock-in.

**Why:** You own your bike and your data. We're just the tool.

## FAQ

### Is Veloce really free?
Yes. Everything—cost analytics, group ride room, service tracking, club discovery—is 100% free forever. We're funded by keeping the product simple and the server costs low. No ads, no tracking, no upsell.

### Can I import my RoadBee logs?
Not yet, but we're building an importer. For now, your bike data in RoadBee is yours—export it and send it to us at support@veloce.app. We'll help you migrate manually.

### How do you make money if it's free?
We charge for premium features we haven't built yet (if they become necessary). For now, we're a lean team using managed cloud services. If you want to support development, we'll have a "Donate" button (not required).

### Is my location data private?
Yes. Your location is only sent to your phone and to your group members when you start a Group Ride Room. We never log it to our servers. Group data deletes 24 hours after the ride ends.

### Can I use this on my iPhone and Android?
We're launching on web first (PWA), then iOS and Android apps. The web version works offline and is installable on your home screen.

### What if I stop using the app?
Export all your data anytime. We don't own your logs.
